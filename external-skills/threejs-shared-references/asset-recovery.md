# Local asset recovery for Tall Tale

Locally adapted from the recovery principles in the upstream Three.js skills
(MIT; license alongside this file). Paid generation and credential workflows
are excluded from this code-only pipeline.

1. Reproduce the failure at its exact shot and timeline time. Record the local
   asset path and browser/loader error before changing the model.
2. Check path and filename case, exported scale/axes, required textures,
   supported materials, and the Three.js version used by the project.
3. Keep the last working asset and source. Repair or re-export the failing
   component; do not replace a user's accepted design without cause.
4. Test the repaired model in a representative shot: silhouette, bounds,
   pivots, materials, and any animation at its start, middle, and end.
5. Run the normal capture check, inspect full-size frames and the contact
   sheet, and report any unresolved defect. Do not describe an untested
   substitute as the original asset.
